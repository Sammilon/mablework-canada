Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2001

Your data protection, absolute confidentiality, and compliance architecture regulations mapped clearly.

1. Compliance Directive Statement

MableWork Canada ("Platform", "We", "Us") operates under strict adherence to the **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)** guidelines alongside provincial-level data handling frameworks. This document establishes how our system architectures process, record, and secure personal profile details, organization registries, and verification file assets.

2. Information Metrics Collected

Our system collects data clusters required to execute professional matching logic and maintain corporate infrastructure security:

  • For Professional Candidates (Workers): Full legal name, date of birth, contact details (phone, email), physical residential location indicators, work experience profiles, preference states, trade certification metrics, uploaded resumes, and government photo identification documents.
  • For Corporate Entities (Companies): Business legal name, federal/provincial business registration credentials, specialized operational categories, company description profiles, website URLs, payment authorization hashes via Stripe, and verified administrator contact personnel parameters.
  • Metadata & Network Logs: Client IP addresses, localized country nodes parsed via local GeoIP mapping, browser configuration states, and interaction data logs captured by our security monitoring arrays.

3. Purpose of Processing and Data Ingestion

We leverage personal and corporate metrics exclusively to:
• Verify individual trade competence and legal eligibility to execute tasks in Canada.
• Facilitate secure real-time WebSocket messaging components between matched platform entities.
• Enforce geographic perimeter security rules, isolating operational systems to authentic Canadian endpoints.
• Route critical ledger alerts, confirmation receipts, and service update notices through verified mail relays.

4. Security Architecture & Asset Retention

All sensitive asset payloads—including resumes and government-issued identifications—are run through validation filters during submission and stored inside secure, access-controlled data arrays using high-grade server-side encryption configurations. Account authorization keys are processed using salted bcrypt hashing routines before database entry to prevent plaintext credential exposure.

Data metrics are retained only for as long as your corporate profile or candidate tracking system remains active on the Platform, or as required by federal Canadian employment history logging regulations.

5. Third-Party Gateway Disclosures

MableWork Canada does not trade, lease, or distribute your identity parameters to third-party marketing brokers. Information is shared strictly with isolated, integrated microservices necessary to run your portal:

  • Stripe Inc: Processes card transaction authorizations directly through secure token frames without exposing banking credentials to our local server environments.
  • Cloudflare: Provides anti-bot verification and connection scrubbing via Turnstile systems to block malicious scrapers and DDoS automated traffic.

6. User Access Rights (PIPEDA Data Access Requests)

Under PIPEDA regulations, Canadian residents maintain explicit rights to request a complete ledger copy of all their personal information elements stored in our data systems, or to ask for corrections of inaccurate details. Account deletion requests will clear your active database profiles, subject to remaining legal financial audit timelines.

For official inquiries regarding information compliance metrics, privacy audits, or specific data deletion requests, email our Privacy Desk: privacy@mablework.ca
