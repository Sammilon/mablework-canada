# MableWork Canada — Employer Operations Guide

Welcome to the MableWork Canada Employer onboarding and management portal manual. This operational guide provides instructions for onboarding your enterprise, making workforce requests, and handling corporate billing clear and efficient.

---

## 1. Corporate Vetting and Compliance

To protect platform integrity and ensure compliance with Canadian employment guidelines, all corporate accounts must pass background vetting before accessing active worker matching services.

### Verification Standards
1. **Document Submission:** Upload official corporate registration credentials or operating licenses via the profile setup interface (`employer-dashboard/company-docs`).
2. **Review Process:** MableWork compliance staff manually review company data within 24 to 48 business hours. You can monitor verification milestones directly on your dashboard.
3. **Sector Assignment:** Your organization will be categorized according to the specific service tracks selected during registration (e.g., Logistics, Manufacturing, Technical Staffing).

---

## 2. Managing Workforce Requests

Once your account is verified, you can dispatch scalable, on-demand staffing requests through the **Workforce Request Console** (`employer-dashboard/workforce-request.html`).

### Creating a Job Order
*   **Target Selection:** Choose the industry discipline and operational skill sets required for your specific workplace placement.
*   **Volume Details:** Define the exact count of personnel needed and the calendar duration (days or weeks) required for the deployment shift.
*   **Operational Directives:** Provide a concise breakdown of duties, location protocols, required equipment, and local site supervisors.

### Tracking and Placements
When your request shifts to `Reviewing` status, our automated matcher and coordination staff map out personnel profiles that match your criteria. Assigned teams are visible inside your **Workforce History Panel** (`employer-dashboard/workforce-history.html`).

---

## 3. Financial Processing and Billing

All corporate transactions, invoice match tracking, and service disbursements are managed through securely encrypted payment gateways.

### Processing Invoices
*   **Gateway Execution:** Corporate accounts process payment transactions via the **Billing Suite** (`employer-dashboard/billing.html`), powered securely by the Stripe API gateway.
*   **Pre-authorization:** Dependent on placement volume parameters, an authorization capture occurs upon staffing allocation to reserve fulfillment personnel.
*   **Ledger Invoicing:** Official itemized receipts can be printed or exported as digital PDFs directly from your transaction history record logs.

---

## 4. Professional Communication Infrastructure

### Real-Time Chat System
Secure client-to-staff interactions happen within the **Messages Suite** (`employer-dashboard/messages.html`). This system lets you coordinate directly with assigned field managers, send shift changes, or handle adjustments immediately.

### Escalation Channels
If a staffing problem or dispute happens on-site, do not handle it through basic worker chats. Open a formal issue ticket in the **Support Terminal** (`employer-dashboard/support.html`) so a platform manager can step in and handle it right away.
